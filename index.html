About Us

Nimikstore is Lokoja's premier destination for fashion and creative design. We specialize in curating stylish clothing, accessories, and offering custom creative design services that help you stand out. Our passion is transforming your fashion ideas into reality, whether you're looking for ready-to-wear pieces or bespoke designs tailored just for you.

Located at Olympic Hotel, Obajana, Kogi State, we serve fashion-forward individuals across Lokoja and beyond with quality, creativity, and exceptional customer service.
